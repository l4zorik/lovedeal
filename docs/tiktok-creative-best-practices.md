# TikTok - Creative Best Practices

## 6 Creative Codes (data-backed)

### 1. TikTok First
- Video musi vypadat jako organic content
- Vertikalni 9:16 format
- Sound-on (90% uzivatelu pouziva zvuk)
- DIY/lo-fi styl (ne prepolisovany)
- Využijte trendy,memes, challenges

### 2. Hook (prvni 3 sekundy)
- 90% ad recall impactu je v prvnich 6 sekundach
- 80% awareness impactu je v prvnich 6 sekundach
- Otevřete pohybem, prekvapenim, otazkou
- Zadne logo intro, zadne pomale build-up
- Silny hook = rozdil mezi uspechem a neuspěchem

### 3. Sound
- TikTok je "sound-on" platforma
- 90% uzivatelu rika ze sound je klicovy
- 15% pravdepodobnost preskoceni bez zvuku
- Hudba pro mood, voiceover pro detaily
- ASMR, product demos = dobre funguji

### 4. Structure (Hook-Body-Close)
```
Hook (0-3s)    -> Pozornost, suspense, prekvapeni
Body (3-20s)   -> Hodnota, branding, storytelling
Close (20-30s) -> Jeden silny CTA
```

### 5. Stimulation
- Text overlays (5-10 slov za sekundu)
- Transitions a stickers
- Pohyb a dynamika
- Emojis a grafika

### 6. Native Style
- Realni lide (creators, zamestnanci, zakaznici)
- Prirozena svetla (ne studio)
- User-generated content styl
- Zadne stock footage

---

## Technical specs for creatives

### Video
- Ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Resolution: min 720p
- Duration: 21-34s optimalni
- Format: .mp4, .mov
- Max size: 500 MB

### Text
- Caption: max 100 znaku (50 pro CN/JP/KR)
- Text overlay: 5-10 slov za sekundu
- Font: bíly, uniformni (bez custom stylu)

### CTA
- Jeden clear CTA na video
- Vizualni i textovy
- Nizke friction ("Koupit", "Vyzkouset")
