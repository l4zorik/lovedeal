# TikTok - Reklamni Formaty

## 1. In-Feed Ads (Auction)
- Format: 9:16 vertical (doporuceno), 16:9, 1:1
- Rozliseni: min 540x960px, doporuceno 720p+
- Delka: 5-60 sekund (doporuceno 9-15s)
- Velikost: max 500 MB
- Bitrate: min 516 kbps

## 2. Spark Ads (Pull)
- Pouziva existujici organicke video
- Zachovava puvodni engagement (likes, comments)
- Autorizace: 7 nebo 30 dni
- Profile: puvodni autor, ne brand

## 3. TopView (Reservation)
- Plnoobrazkovy pri otevreni aplikace
- Max 60 sekund (doporuceno 9-15s)
- Jeden impression na uzivatele za den
- Vhodne pro velke launches

## 4. Brand Takeover (Reservation)
- 3-5 sekundovy full-screen ad
- Zobrazen pri otevreni aplikace
- CPM: ~$20-50+ (premium)
- Jeden impression na uzivatele za den

## 5. Branded Hashtag Challenge (Reservation)
- UGC format - uzivatele vytvareji obsah
- Max 60 sekund video
- Vhodne pro engagement a viralitu

## 6. Image Ads
- 1:1 ratio
- Max 50 KB
- Auction format

## Technicke specifikace

### Video requirements
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
- Resolution: >= 540x960px (720p+ recommended)
- Duration: 5-60s (9-15s recommended)
- File size: <= 500 MB
- Bitrate: >= 516 kbps
- Format: .mp4, .mov, .mpeg, .3gp, .avi

### Profile photo
- Dimension: 98x98px, Square 1:1
- Format: .jpg, .jpeg, .png
- File size: < 50 KB
- Safe zone: 66x66px center

### Safe zone
- Velikost zavisi na caption delce
- Klicove prvky musi byt v centru
- Stahujte safe zone sablony z TikTok Ads Manager
